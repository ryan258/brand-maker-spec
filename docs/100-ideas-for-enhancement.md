# Personal Brand OS Enhancement Roadmap

A backlog of concrete improvements for the personal brand operating system. The
product serves one owner across raw ideas, named concepts, and existing projects.
Quick generation is an entry path into the same living workspace, not a separate
parody product.

Each remaining item is marked **Build**, **Adapt**, or **Defer**. Build items fit the
confirmed product directly. Adapt items keep their value without multi-user or SaaS
machinery. Deferred items are retained for historical context but are outside the
current local-first, single-owner scope. See `docs/specs/personal-brand-os-roadmap.md`
for the rationale and `docs/specs/personal-brand-os-implementation-plan.md` for order.
Completed ideas retain their original numbers in `changelog.md`.
